# Rams scorecard

Scoring uses 0–3 per principle. When evidence was incomplete, the lower defensible score was chosen.

1. **Good design is innovative — 2/3.** Direct manipulation, cross-highlighting and the feature/role/sprint model are meaningfully tailored to this product, but the interaction is pointer-only and the visual novelty currently relies heavily on decorative motion.
2. **Good design makes a product useful — 3/3.** The product supports the primary planning loop end to end: create a feature, assign roles, place work in dated sprints, inspect capacity, filter and persist.
3. **Good design is aesthetic — 2/3.** Ceramic styling has a coherent material direction and strong hierarchy at macro scale, but contrast failures and 29 concurrent loops weaken composition and polish.
4. **Good design makes a product understandable — 1/3.** Ambiguous view labels, FTE described as people, jargon, pointer-only affordances and metadata density make important behavior harder to infer.
5. **Good design is unobtrusive — 1/3.** Persistent glints, ambient motion, a crowded feature header and a busy load header compete with the schedule instead of receding behind it.
6. **Good design is honest — 1/3.** Save/destructive states are honest, but capacity terminology, audience-style view labels and inaccessible visual affordances do not accurately communicate behavior.
7. **Good design is long-lasting — 1/3.** The data model is durable, but the current high-gloss/neumorphic treatment, year-specific effect label and animation density risk aging quickly.
8. **Good design is thorough down to the last detail — 1/3.** Many application states are covered, yet focus, landmarks, dialog semantics, contrast and core keyboard equivalents have substantial gaps.
9. **Good design is environmentally friendly — 1/3.** Reduced-motion support is good, but a 571 KB single JavaScript chunk and 29 idle loops are costly for a focused planning tool.
10. **Good design is as little design as possible — 1/3.** Three duplicated affordance groups, low-value metadata, three zoom choices and multiple simultaneous visual effects can be removed without weakening the core task.

**Total: 14/30**
